1. WHAT DOES A STUDENT DO IN THIS SYSTEM?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

As a student, this system lets you:

  • Log in with your personal username and password
  • Enter an access token (given by your teacher) to start an exam
  • Answer all the questions and submit your exam
  • Immediately see your score as soon as you finish
  • Come back later to review every answer with feedback explanations
  • See a history of all exams you have ever taken

That is all you can do as a student. You cannot see other students' answers,
create questions, change your own account details, or access any teacher or
admin pages.

2. WHAT YOU NEED BEFORE YOU START
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Before sitting an exam, make sure you have ALL three of these:

  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  1. USERNAME    — given to you by your teacher (e.g. STUDENT1)           │
  │  2. PASSWORD    — given to you by your teacher (e.g. Student123)         │
  │  3. ACCESS TOKEN — written on the board or given on exam day              │
  │                   (e.g. DEMO-2026)                                        │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

If you do not have all three, ask your teacher before the exam starts.

IMPORTANT ABOUT YOUR USERNAME:
  The system automatically stores usernames in UPPERCASE.
  Even if your teacher wrote "student1" in lowercase, you can type it either
  way — the system will match it. If in doubt, type it exactly as it appears
  on the sheet your teacher gave you.

3. HOW TO LOG IN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  1. Open a web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari).
  2. Type the school's exam website address in the address bar and press Enter.
  3. You will see a white card in the middle of the screen:

       ╔══════════════════════╗
       ║  Exam Dashboard      ║
       ║  Sign in with your   ║
       ║  account to continue.║
       ║                      ║
       ║  Username: [______]  ║
       ║  Password: [______]  ║
       ║                      ║
       ║  [  Sign in  ]       ║
       ╚══════════════════════╝

  4. Click inside the "Username" box and type your username.
  5. Click inside the "Password" box and type your password.
  6. Click the green "Sign in" button.
  7. If the username and password are correct, you go straight to your
     Home screen.

WHAT IF IT SAYS "WRONG PASSWORD" OR WON'T LET YOU IN?
  → Check you have typed the username exactly as given (no extra spaces).
  → Check the password is typed correctly (passwords are case-sensitive —
    "Student123" is different from "student123").
  → If you are still stuck, ask your teacher to reset your password.

4. YOUR HOME SCREEN — TWO CHOICES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

After you sign in, you land on your Home screen. It is very simple:

  ╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
  ║  Welcome, [Your Full Name]                                       ║
  ║  What would you like to do?                                      ║
  ║                                                                  ║
  ║  ┌────────────────────────┐  ┌──────────────────────────────┐   ║
  ║  │  Take an exam          │  │  View my scores              │   ║
  ║  │  Enter the access      │  │  See every exam you've       │   ║
  ║  │  token from your       │  │  already taken, with         │   ║
  ║  │  teacher and start     │  │  detailed question-by-       │   ║
  ║  │  the exam.             │  │  question review.            │   ║
  ║  └────────────────────────┘  └──────────────────────────────┘   ║
  ║                                                                  ║
  ║  [Sign out]                                                      ║
  ╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

  TAKE AN EXAM → Click this to start a new exam. You will need your token.
  VIEW MY SCORES → Click this to see results from exams you have already done.
  SIGN OUT → Click this to safely log out.

This home screen is always accessible during your session. If you get lost
at any point, look for a "Home" or "Back" link at the top of the page.

5. VIEWING YOUR SCORES (BEFORE TAKING ANY EXAM)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Click "View my scores" from the Home screen.

Page title: "My scores — Exams you've already taken, newest first."

If you have not taken any exams yet, the page shows:
  "You haven't submitted any exams yet."
  [Take an exam] button — click it to go straight to the token entry page.

If you have taken exams, they appear in a table. The table has these columns:

  EXAM       — the exam name and short code (e.g., "Demo Exam" / DEMO)
  STARTED    — the date and time you began the exam
  SUBMITTED  — the date and time you clicked "Submit exam"
  SCORE      — your percentage score and raw points (e.g., 71.43 / 100,
               Raw 15/21)
  STATUS     — whether your result is Final (all grading done) or
               Pending (essay questions not yet graded by teacher)
  REVIEW     — a "Review" link to see full question-by-question feedback

NAVIGATION on this page:
  [Back]     — goes back to your Home screen
  [Sign out] — logs you out

6. ENTERING AN EXAM — THE TOKEN PAGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Click "Take an exam" from the Home screen (or "Take an exam" from the scores
page). You arrive at the Token Entry page.

WHAT THIS PAGE LOOKS LIKE:

  LEFT SIDE (the form):
  ╔═══════════════════════════════════╗
  ║  Exam Dashboard                   ║
  ║  Welcome, Student One.            ║
  ║  Enter the exam token provided    ║
  ║  by your teacher.                 ║
  ║                                   ║
  ║  Exam token: [________________]   ║
  ║                                   ║
  ║  [  Start exam  ]                 ║
  ║                                   ║
  ║  [Sign out]                       ║
  ╚═══════════════════════════════════╝

  RIGHT SIDE (helpful notes):
    ❷  Different exam, different token. Make sure the token matches
       the exam you're about to take.

    15s  Answers are saved automatically on every change and every
         15 seconds.

    1×   The token binds to your account on validation. Do not
         share it with other students.

STEPS:
  1. Look at the board (or your printed sheet) for the access token.
     Example: DEMO-2026
  2. Click inside the "Exam token" box.
  3. Type the token exactly as given. It is usually UPPERCASE with a dash.
  4. Click the green "Start exam" button.
  5. The exam loads immediately.

WHAT IF IT SAYS "INVALID TOKEN"?
  → Check you typed the token correctly (no extra spaces, correct dashes).
  → The token might have expired — ask your teacher for a new one.
  → The token might have used up all its allowed uses — ask your teacher.
  → Make sure you are entering the correct token for today's exam.

WHAT IF IT SAYS "TOKEN ALREADY USED"?
  → You may have already started this exam.
  → The system automatically resumes your previous session.
  → Your answers are safely stored. Continue where you left off.

7. THE EXAM SCREEN — WHAT EVERYTHING MEANS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Once the exam starts, the screen is divided into three areas:

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TOP BAR (always visible)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  [Exam name]   [X / Y answered · Saved]   [TIMER]

  Exam name   — the name of your exam (e.g., "Demo Exam")
  X / Y answered — how many questions you have answered so far out of total
                   (e.g., "3 / 5 answered")
  "Saved"     — a green dot and the word "Saved" appears after each answer
                is safely stored. This confirms your answer is NOT lost.
  TIMER       — counts down from the total exam time (e.g., "29:48")
                Watch this — when it reaches 0:00, the exam auto-submits.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS BANNER (yellow strip, top of main area)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Shows any instructions your teacher typed, e.g.:
  "Answer all questions. Good luck!"

  This banner is always visible as you work through the exam.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
MAIN QUESTION AREA (centre of screen)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Shows ONE question at a time. The question card contains:

  Question N · [Topic] · [X] pts
    N      = question number (1, 2, 3 …)
    Topic  = the subject area of the question (e.g., "Arithmetic")
    X pts  = how many marks this question is worth

  The question text (what you are being asked)

  The answer input (different for each question type — see Section 8)

  At the bottom of the question card:
    [← Previous]   — go to the previous question
    [Next →]        — go to the next question

  NOTE: On the LAST question, the "Next →" button is replaced by
        "Submit exam". This is your final chance to review before submitting.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RIGHT SIDEBAR (always visible on the right)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  "Questions" heading, then numbered buttons: [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

  → Click any number to jump directly to that question.
  → Buttons that have been answered may appear differently (highlighted
    or filled) so you can see at a glance which ones you have done.

  At the bottom of the sidebar:
    [Submit exam] — click to submit whenever you are ready.
    You can submit before using up all your time.

8. ANSWERING QUESTION TYPES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

There are five different question types you may see. Here is how to answer
each one:

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8a. SINGLE CHOICE (Multiple Choice — pick exactly one answer)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  How it looks:
    A question followed by 3–6 options. Each option has a round radio
    button (○) to its left.

  Example:
    What is 2 + 2?
    ○  3
    ○  4    ← click here to select this answer
    ○  5
    ○  6

  How to answer:
    1. Read all the options.
    2. Click the round button (○) next to the option you think is correct.
       The button fills in (●) and the row turns blue/highlighted.
    3. You can change your mind — just click a different option.
       Only ONE option can be selected at a time; selecting a new one
       automatically deselects the previous one.

  How it is graded:
    Automatic — right or wrong, full marks or zero.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8b. MULTI-SELECT (Tick all that apply — pick one or more answers)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  How it looks:
    A question followed by options with square checkboxes (☐) to the left.

  Example:
    Select the even numbers.
    ☑  2    ← ticked (selected)
    ☐  3
    ☑  4    ← ticked (selected)
    ☐  5

  How to answer:
    1. Read the question carefully. More than one answer may be correct.
    2. Click the checkbox (☐) next to EACH option you want to select.
       A tick (☑) appears and the row turns blue.
    3. To deselect an option, click its checkbox again — the tick disappears.
    4. You can tick as many or as few options as you think are correct.

  How it is graded:
    Partial credit. You earn more points for each correct selection
    and lose points for each wrong selection. Selecting no answers = 0.

  TIP: Do NOT just tick everything. Wrong ticks reduce your score.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8c. SHORT TEXT (Type a word or short phrase)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  How it looks:
    A question followed by a single-line text input box.

  Example:
    What is the capital of France?
    [Paris________________________________]

  How to answer:
    1. Click inside the text box.
    2. Type your answer.
    3. The box turns blue/highlighted to show it is active.
    4. Your answer saves automatically — you do not need to press Enter.

  How it is graded:
    Automatic. The system checks if your text matches the correct answer
    (not case-sensitive, so "paris" and "PARIS" are both accepted).
    Spelling matters. "Pariss" would be marked wrong.

  TIP: Keep it brief — just the word or phrase the question asks for.
       Do not add extra words like "The answer is …".

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8d. NUMERIC (Type a number)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  How it looks:
    A question followed by a number input box (with small up/down arrows
    on the side — called a "spinner").

  Example:
    What is 6 × 7?
    [42    ▲]
            ▼

  How to answer:
    1. Click inside the number box.
    2. Type the number. Only type the number itself (no units, no letters).
       For example: type  42  not  "42 metres".
    3. You can also click the small ▲ (up) or ▼ (down) arrows to increase
       or decrease the number one step at a time.
    4. Your answer saves automatically.

  How it is graded:
    Automatic, with tolerance bands:
      → Exact answer = 100% of the marks
      → Within 1%    = 100% of the marks
      → Within 5%    = 80% of the marks
      → Within 10%   = 50% of the marks
      → Further off  = 0 marks
    So a very close estimate still earns partial credit.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8e. ESSAY / STRUCTURED (Write a full answer in your own words)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  How it looks:
    A question followed by a large text area (a big open box for writing).

  Example:
    State Newton's first law of motion.
    ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │ An object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion   │
    │ stays in motion at constant velocity, unless acted upon    │
    │ by an external force.                                      │
    │                                                            │
    └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  How to answer:
    1. Click inside the large text area.
    2. Type your full answer. You can write multiple sentences or paragraphs.
    3. The box grows taller automatically if you write a lot.
    4. Your answer saves as you type — no need to click anything.

  How it is graded:
    NOT automatic. Your teacher reads your answer and gives you a score
    and sometimes written feedback. This may take days after the exam.
    Your overall result will show "Pending" until the teacher finishes.

  TIP: Write in full sentences. Give as much detail as the question asks for.
       A one-word answer for a 6-point essay will score very low.

9. MOVING BETWEEN QUESTIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

You have THREE ways to move between questions:

  1. [← Previous] button (bottom-left of the question card)
       Goes back one question. Your current answer is saved first.
       This button is greyed out / inactive on Question 1 (you cannot go
       back from the first question).

  2. [Next →] button (bottom-right of the question card)
       Goes forward one question. Your current answer is saved first.
       On the LAST question, this button says "Submit exam" instead.

  3. SIDEBAR NUMBER BUTTONS [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]
       Visible on the right side of the screen at all times.
       Click any number to jump directly to that question.
       Very useful for reviewing your work before submitting.

IMPORTANT:
  → Your answers are NOT lost when you navigate. The system saves everything
    automatically each time you leave a question.
  → You CAN go back and change answers on previous questions.
  → There is no penalty for going back to revisit and change answers.

10. THE TIMER — HOW IT WORKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The timer is shown in the top-right corner of the screen at all times:

  29:48  ← minutes:seconds remaining

EXAM MODES — the timer behaves differently depending on your exam type:

  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  STRICT MODE (real exam / important test)                           │
  │    → The timer counts down continuously, even if you:              │
  │      - close the browser tab                                        │
  │      - lose your internet connection                                │
  │      - turn off your computer (then come back)                      │
  │    → When time runs out, the exam submits AUTOMATICALLY.            │
  │    → You CANNOT pause or stop the clock.                           │
  │    → Use your time wisely — it is running from the moment you       │
  │      enter the token.                                               │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  TRY OUT MODE (practice exam / homework)                            │
  │    → The timer PAUSES when you close the browser tab.               │
  │    → It RESUMES when you come back to the tab.                      │
  │    → This mode is for self-paced practice — no pressure.            │
  │    → After submitting, you see full explanations for every answer.  │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Your teacher decides which mode each exam uses. If you are unsure, ask them.

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN TIME RUNS OUT (STRICT MODE):
  → The exam is automatically submitted with whatever answers you have given.
  → If you are in the middle of typing when time runs out, that partial
    answer is still saved.
  → You cannot add any more answers after auto-submission.
  → You are taken directly to the Results page.

TIMER TIP: Keep an eye on the timer, especially in the last 5 minutes.
If you have unanswered questions, use the sidebar number buttons to jump
directly to them rather than clicking "Next" one at a time.

11. HOW YOUR ANSWERS ARE SAVED AUTOMATICALLY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

You do NOT need to press a "Save" button. The system saves your work in
two ways:

  INSTANTLY — every time you select a radio button, tick a checkbox,
  or type a character, the answer is saved to your browser immediately.

  EVERY 15 SECONDS — every 15 seconds, all your answers are also sent
  to the school's server and stored safely in the database.

  You know saving has worked when you see:
    "X / Y answered · Saved"  in the top bar
    The word "Saved" appears in green/teal colour.

WHAT HAPPENS IF MY INTERNET CUTS OUT?
  → Your answers stay saved in your browser.
  → When internet is restored, they automatically sync to the server.
  → The word "Saved" in the top bar may temporarily change to
    "Saving…" — wait a moment for it to return to "Saved".
  → In Strict mode, the timer kept running during your disconnection,
    so some time may have passed.

WHAT HAPPENS IF MY BROWSER CRASHES OR I CLOSE THE TAB?
  → Your answers are already safely stored on the server.
  → When you return to the exam website, log in, and enter your token again,
    the system will detect that you were in the middle of an exam.
  → You will see a "Resume" prompt — click it to continue exactly where
    you left off with all answers intact.

This means your answers will NEVER be lost due to technical problems.

12. SUBMITTING THE EXAM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

When you are ready to hand in your exam, click "Submit exam".

There are TWO places to find the "Submit exam" button:

  1. In the RIGHT SIDEBAR — visible on every question throughout the exam.
  2. At the BOTTOM of the last question — replaces the "Next →" button
     when you reach question 5 (or the last question).

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU CLICK "SUBMIT EXAM":
  → The exam is finalised — no more changes can be made.
  → Your answers are locked in.
  → The system scores your multiple-choice, short-text, and numeric answers
    automatically and immediately.
  → You are taken to the Results page (see Section 13).

NOTE: You can submit BEFORE the timer runs out. If you finish early,
click "Submit exam" and you are done. You do not have to wait.

SHOULD I SUBMIT IF I HAVEN'T ANSWERED EVERYTHING?
  → You can submit with unanswered questions — the system accepts it.
  → Unanswered questions score 0 points.
  → It is always better to attempt an answer than to leave it blank.
  → In Strict mode: if time runs out, submission is automatic regardless.

16. SIGNING OUT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

You will see a "Sign out" button in three places:

  → On your Home screen (bottom of the page)
  → On the Token entry page (below the "Start exam" button)
  → On the Scores page (top-right corner)

IMPORTANT: Always sign out when you finish, especially on a shared computer.
If you close the browser tab without signing out, you may still be logged in
when someone else opens the website.

If you are taking an exam and accidentally click "Sign out":
  → Do NOT panic. Your answers are already saved on the server.
  → Log back in immediately.
  → Enter the same token again.
  → The system will detect your previous session and ask if you want to
    "Resume" — click Resume to continue exactly where you left off.

17. IMPORTANT RULES AND TIPS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

★ DO NOT SHARE YOUR TOKEN — The token binds to your account the moment you
  use it. If another student uses the same token on your account, you may
  be locked out or flagged for cheating. Always keep your token private.

★ DO NOT SHARE YOUR USERNAME AND PASSWORD — These are personal and identify
  you in the system. All submissions made with your credentials are
  recorded under your name.

★ IN STRICT MODE, THE CLOCK NEVER STOPS — Even if you close the tab, lose
  internet, or your battery dies. The exam keeps timing on the server.
  Come back as fast as possible if something goes wrong.

★ YOUR ANSWERS ARE NEVER LOST — The system saves to your browser
  instantly and to the server every 15 seconds. There is no way to
  lose work from a crash or disconnection.

★ ESSAY RESULTS TAKE TIME — If your exam has essay questions, your overall
  result shows "Pending" until your teacher manually reads and grades your
  essay. This can take a few days. Check back on "View my scores" to see
  when it updates to "Final".

★ YOU CAN REVIEW AFTER SUBMITTING — In Try Out (practice) mode, you see
  full explanations for every answer right after submission. In Strict mode,
  you only see your score and topic breakdown — not the explanations.

★ LEAVING THE TAB DURING A STRICT EXAM — The system records when you
  switch away from the exam tab. Your teacher may see this in a security
  log. This does not immediately lock you out, but it is noted.

★ CHANGE YOUR ANSWERS BEFORE SUBMITTING — You can change any answer up
  until the moment you click "Submit exam" (or time runs out). Use the
  sidebar number buttons to jump back to any question and change your answer.

★ PARTIAL CREDIT IS POSSIBLE — For multi-select and numeric questions, you
  can earn partial marks even if your answer is not completely right. Always
  attempt these questions — a partial answer is better than a blank.

★ PASSWORDS ARE CASE-SENSITIVE — "Student123" and "student123" are
  different passwords. If login fails, check your Caps Lock key.

★ USERNAMES ARE NOT CASE-SENSITIVE — The system accepts your username in
  any combination of upper/lowercase letters.
